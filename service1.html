Frequently Asked

Questions families often ask.

Find quick answers about visits, support and family updates.

What does a companion visit include?

Visits can include conversation, reading, games, walks, simple activities, companionship and other agreed forms of everyday support.

Can families receive updates after visits?

Yes. Visit summaries and relevant observations can be organized so families can stay informed about completed support.

Can assistance include errands?

Errand assistance can include groceries, small household errands or accompanying an elderly person outside, depending on the agreed service.

Can a companion assist with appointments?

Appointment assistance can include accompanying an elderly person to doctor visits or other appointments as arranged.

Can families see caregiver assignments?

The family experience can provide caregiver assignment details so families know who is scheduled to provide support.
